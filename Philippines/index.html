Palawan Underground River

Known as Puerto Princesa Underground River, this 8.2 km subterranean marvel winds through breathtaking limestone cliffs, crystal-clear waters, and cathedral-like cave formations — a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the New Seven Wonders of Nature.

Mayon Volcano

The world's most perfectly shaped stratovolcano, rising 2,462 m above Luzon Island. Mayon is both awe-inspiring and humbling — a reminder of the raw geological forces shaping the Philippine archipelago.
