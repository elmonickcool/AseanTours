Highlights of Cambodia

01
Iconic Landmark

Angkor Wat

The largest religious monument in the world, built in the 12th century and a proud symbol of Cambodia. Its stunning architecture, intricate carvings, and UNESCO status make it unmissable — a place where history and spirituality converge at sunrise.

Plan Your Visit
02
Jungle Temple

Ta Prohm — the Tomb Raider Temple

Giant tree roots weave through ancient stone in this hauntingly beautiful Bayon-style temple. Built in the late 12th century, Ta Prohm feels like a world reclaimed by nature.

Explore
03
Capital City

Phnom Penh

A city of contrasts — the gilded Royal Palace beside the somber Tuol Sleng museum, bustling markets alongside peaceful riverside promenades. Cambodia's living, breathing heart.

Explore
04
Night Experience

Angkor Night Market, Siem Reap

After dark, Siem Reap comes alive. Browse hand-crafted souvenirs, silk garments, and local artisan goods. Taste Cambodian street food, sip fresh coconuts, and lose yourself in the warm glow and sounds of this beloved outdoor bazaar.
